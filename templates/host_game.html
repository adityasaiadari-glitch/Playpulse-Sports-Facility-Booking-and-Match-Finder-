Host a game

We'll book the court for you and list the game so players can join and split the cost.

Must be a sport offered at the chosen venue (icons shown next to each venue).

How hosting works

  1. We check the slot is free and book one court for 1 hour.
  2. Your game appears on “Find a game” for players to join.
  3. The court price is split equally between all players.
  4. Cancel the booking from “My bookings” to cancel the game.
